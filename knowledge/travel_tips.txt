General Travel Planning Guidelines:

Budget Estimation:
- Budget accommodation: $30-60/night
- Mid-range accommodation: $80-150/night
- Luxury accommodation: $200+/night
- Daily food budget: $20-50 per person (varies by destination)
- Local transportation: $10-30/day
- Activities and attractions: $20-100/day

Best Times to Travel:
- Shoulder seasons (spring/fall) often offer best value
- Book flights 2-3 months in advance for best prices
- Tuesday and Wednesday flights are typically cheaper
- Avoid peak holiday periods for better prices

Packing Essentials:
- Travel documents (passport, visa, travel insurance)
- Universal power adapter
- First aid kit and medications
- Comfortable walking shoes
- Weather-appropriate clothing
- Portable charger and cables
- Copy of important documents

Safety Tips:
- Register with embassy for international travel
- Keep digital and physical copies of documents
- Use hotel safe for valuables
- Be aware of common scams in tourist areas
- Have emergency contacts saved
- Get travel insurance

Cultural Considerations:
- Research local customs and etiquette
- Learn basic phrases in local language
- Respect dress codes at religious sites
- Understand tipping customs
- Be mindful of photography restrictions

Health Precautions:
- Check vaccination requirements
- Drink bottled water in uncertain areas
- Use sunscreen and insect repellent
- Know location of nearby hospitals
- Carry basic medications
